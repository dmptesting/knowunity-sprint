# Radius

Every `Radius` token applied to a 96×96px box, smallest to largest — `Radius.Full` renders as a circle, which is
the correct result of applying a radius larger than the box, not a special case. `Radius` tokens have no
`$description` field in tokens.json, which is called out on each box rather than left blank.
